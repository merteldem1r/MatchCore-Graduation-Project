# Academic Integrity & Data Rules

## Data and code

1. **No proprietary material.** No code, data, documents or internal designs from any current or previous employer. Not even "from memory" reconstructions of internal systems.
2. **Data**: synthetic workloads we generate, or openly licensed public data. Check and record the license in [`../research/resources.md`](../research/resources.md).
3. **Third-party code**: only with a compatible license, clearly credited. The core matching engine is our own work.

## Writing (proposal, interim report, thesis)

4. Theses are checked with **Turnitin**. Text must be written **in our own words**. Notes in `docs/steps/` (including AI-assisted drafts) are study material and must be rewritten, not pasted.
5. Direct quotations go in **quotation marks** with a citation. A citation alone does not make copied text acceptable.
6. Every source used is cited: numeric style `[1]` or Harvard style, following the thesis writing guide (`../graduation-project/templates/Project-Thesis-Writing-Guide.docx`).
7. Follow the formatting rules of the writing guide: Times New Roman 12; margins 3 / 2 / 2.5 / 2.5 cm; figures and tables numbered as `Şekil 3.1` / `Tablo 3.1`; equations as `(4.2)`.

## Privacy

8. No personal data in the repository (student number, private emails, others' names without consent).
