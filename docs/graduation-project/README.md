## Deadlines (fall week 1 = Mon 2026-09-14)

| When | What | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **By end of fall week 7 → Sun 2026-11-01** | **Proposal form** (*Proje Başvuru Formu*) | Email to `bm@medeniyet.edu.tr`, subject `BIL497: proje başvuru formu`, advisor CC'd (then no signature needed) |
| 2026-11-02 → 11-08 | Midterm week | — |
| **By fall week 14 → ~2026-12-14 to 12-20** | **Interim report** (*Ara Rapor*), if the advisor requests it. BIL 497 grade is based on it | Send to the advisor |
| 2026-12-25 | Classes end (finals 12-26 → 2027-01-05) | — |
| **Spring 2027, end of week 13** (calendar not published yet) | **Thesis** (*Bitirme Tezi*), Turnitin-checked | Email to `bm@medeniyet.edu.tr`, subject `BIL498: bitirme tezi`, copy to the advisor |
| End of spring 2027 | **Jury presentation**: 3 faculty members, 10 min talk + 5 min Q&A | Date announced by the department |

Weekly ~30-minute advisor meetings are expected, and the student arranges them.

## What each document asks for

| Document | Sections |
| --- | --- |
| **Proposal form** | Title (TR + EN) · members · date · advisor · *Projenin Amacı* (~½ page) · *Yöntem ve Metodlar* incl. software/hardware tools · *İş zaman çizelgesi* for both semesters · numbered references |
| **Interim report** | Problem & importance · goals · similar work · scope (done / to do) · methods & tools · timeline, success factors, progress · project management · references |
| **Thesis** | Önsöz · İçindekiler · Tablo/Şekil listesi · Özet + Abstract · Giriş · Genel Kısımlar · Materyal ve Yöntem · Bulgular · Tartışma ve Sonuç · Kaynaklar · Ekler |
| **Evaluation form** (jury, 100 pts) | Goals 5 · literature 5 · fit to programme 5 · ethics 5 · methods 10 · originality 10 · problem modelling 10 · interpreting results 10 · formatting 10 · references 10 · presentation 4×5 = 20 |
