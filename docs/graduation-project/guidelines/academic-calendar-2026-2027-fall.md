## Notes for AI Agents

- All dates are in the Gregorian calendar and follow the `YYYY-MM-DD` format.
- Dates are **calendar dates**, not timestamps; the source does not specify times or time zones.
- Start and end dates are inclusive.
- A deadline with identical start and end dates is a single-day event.
- This calendar applies to the **2026–2027 fall semester only**. It does not include the spring semester.
- This document is the **general undergraduate academic calendar**, not a graduation-project-specific submission schedule. Graduation project proposal, interim report, and thesis deadlines must be tracked separately using the department's guidelines.
- When the source lists only dates, do not infer a specific time of day for a deadline.
