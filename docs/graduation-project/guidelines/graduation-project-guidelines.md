## 2026–2027 Graduation Project Notes

### Graduation Theses

- Write your thesis in accordance with the formatting requirements, instructions, writing guide, and your advisor's requirements provided on the [official graduation project page](https://bm.medeniyet.edu.tr/tr/egitim/bitirme-projesi).
- Pay particular attention to references and citations, following the rules set out in the thesis writing guide.
  - Do not directly copy and paste material from any source without clearly identifying it as a direct quotation.
  - Even when you cite the source, copied text may still constitute plagiarism unless the directly quoted material is enclosed in quotation marks.
  - Write your thesis in your own words and provide appropriate citations to the sources you use.
  - Theses will be checked for plagiarism using **Turnitin**. Theses that do not pass the plagiarism check will not be accepted.
- Submit your thesis by the announced deadline to **[bm@medeniyet.edu.tr](mailto:bm@medeniyet.edu.tr)**, using the email subject **`BIL497: Bitirme tezi`** (or **`BIL498: Bitirme tezi`**, as applicable), and **CC your project advisor**.

### Graduation Project Presentations

- Graduation theses will be presented before a jury of **three faculty members**, including the project advisor.
- The examination grade will be calculated as the **arithmetic mean of the grades awarded by the three jury members**.
- Presentations will take place in the rooms and on the dates announced by the department.
- Each group will have a maximum of **15 minutes**:
  - **10 minutes** for the presentation.
  - **5 minutes** for questions.
- You may structure your presentation around the sections of your thesis.

## General Information

The graduation project is normally intended to be undertaken in the **fourth year** of the undergraduate curriculum. However, students may begin their projects in their **third year**. The project is assessed through the **BIL 497** and **BIL 498** courses.

In general, a graduation project may involve:

- Developing software or hardware to solve a problem or make a task easier.
- Producing a mathematical proof using existing computational or software tools.

You may choose any project topic related to computer engineering. Discuss your proposed topic with department faculty members or your project advisor to determine how to proceed.

Students are expected to carry out and complete the technical work during their final year. When planning your course load, keep in mind that the time required for the project may account for **up to one-third of your total semester workload**.

You are expected to meet with your project advisor for approximately **30 minutes per week**. Make effective use of these meetings and take the initiative to arrange them rather than waiting for your advisor to contact you.

### Defining the Project Scope

When selecting a project, first identify an area of computer engineering that interests you. Review previous work in that area and clearly define the problem you intend to address, whether by developing a different solution or adopting a similar approach.

Ideally, your completed project should provide at least a **partial solution** to the problem you have identified.

### Selecting Technologies

In your graduation thesis, explain why you selected the **technologies, methods, and algorithms** used in your project. A detailed comparison with alternative technologies is **not required**.

### Unexpected Circumstances

If your project does not progress as expected, consult your advisor about changing the project or pursuing a different approach. For other types of difficulties, you may also seek assistance from the department administration.

## Project Procedures

Students are expected to enroll in the graduation project courses during **both semesters**: **BIL 497** and **BIL 498**. BIL 497 primarily covers the initial phase of the project. Follow the procedures outlined below.

### Choosing a Topic and Submitting the Proposal Form

1. Review the graduation project topics announced by faculty members, conduct preliminary research into your preferred topic, and discuss it with the relevant faculty member.
2. If you have your own project idea, you may pursue it provided that a faculty member working in a relevant field agrees to supervise and approve the proposed topic.
3. Once the topic and advisor have been determined, complete the [Project Proposal Form](https://bm.medeniyet.edu.tr/documents/bm/Mezuniyet%20Projesi/proje-oneri-formu.docx).
4. Submit the completed proposal form to the department administration **by the seventh week of the Fall semester**.

**Email submission instructions:**

- **Recipient:** [bm@medeniyet.edu.tr](mailto:bm@medeniyet.edu.tr)
- **Subject:** `BIL497: proje başvuru formu` (or `BIL498: proje başvuru formu`, as applicable)
- If your advisor sends the submission or is CC'd on the email, **an additional signature on the document is not required**.

### Completing the Project

- Carry out the required project work under the supervision of your advisor throughout both semesters.
- At the end of the Fall semester, your advisor may request a [Project Interim Report](https://bm.medeniyet.edu.tr/documents/bm/Mezuniyet%20Projesi/ara_rapor.docx) for **BIL 497**.
  - If requested, submit the report describing the work completed up to the **14th week of the Fall semester** to your advisor.
  - **The BIL 497 grade is assigned by the advisor based on the evaluation of submitted reports.**
- Complete the remaining project work by the end of the **Spring semester**.

### Writing and Submitting the Graduation Thesis

The **BIL 498** grade is based on completion of the project by the end of the Spring semester, submission of the graduation thesis, and participation in the project presentation.

At the end of the Spring semester, prepare a [Graduation Project Thesis](https://bm.medeniyet.edu.tr/documents/bm/Mezuniyet%20Projesi/bitirme_projesi_tezi.docx) describing:

- The work carried out during the project.
- The methods and tools used.
- The use and operation of the software and hardware developed.
- The results obtained.

Follow the requirements in the [Thesis Writing Guide](https://bm.medeniyet.edu.tr/documents/bm/Mezuniyet%20Projesi/yazim-klavuzu.docx).

**Thesis submission instructions:**

- **Recipient:** [bm@medeniyet.edu.tr](mailto:bm@medeniyet.edu.tr)
- **Subject:** `BIL498: bitirme tezi`
- **Additional requirement:** Provide a copy of the thesis to your project advisor.
- **Deadline:** The thesis is expected to be completed **by the end of the 13th week of the Spring semester**, before the semester ends.

At the end of the semester, present your thesis work before the graduation project jury on the dates determined by the department administration.

Following the presentation, the jury and project advisor assess BIL 498 using the [Project Evaluation Form](https://bm.medeniyet.edu.tr/documents/bm/Mezuniyet%20Projesi/degerlendirme_formu.docx).

### Suggested Project Topics

The department lists the following suggested project areas:

1. Big data analytics and data mining.
2. Machine learning-based solutions.
3. Machine learning and IoT device control.
4. Mobile tracking systems and applications.
5. Network and information security.
6. Quantum machine learning and optimization applications.

**Other topics may be selected with the approval of a project advisor.**

### Eligible Project Advisors

Faculty members holding the academic ranks of **Assistant Professor (Dr. Öğretim Üyesi)**, **Associate Professor (Doçent)**, or **Professor (Profesör)** may be selected as graduation project advisors.

See the department's [Academic Staff Directory](https://bm.medeniyet.edu.tr/tr/personel/akademik).

### Additional Notes

- The details of each project will be determined jointly by the student and the project advisor.
- Students may apply as groups of **two or three**. Larger groups may be permitted for larger projects.
- For group projects, the thesis must document the **division of work** and the responsibilities of each group member.
